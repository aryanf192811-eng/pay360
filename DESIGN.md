## Brand & Style
The PeoplePay360 Design System explicitly rejects "generic AI" glassmorphism. It uses a highly premium, rigid corporate aesthetic inspired by the Odoo HRMS 24-hours reference.

## Colors
- **Primary (#3062e1):** Strong Blue for actions and highlights.
- **Background (#faf6dc):** A distinctive, premium cream/off-white background.
- **Surface (#fefefe):** Stark pure white for cards and high-contrast containers.
- **Text (#454134):** Dark Gray/Slate for primary text.
- **Muted (#91918d):** Mid Gray for secondary text.

## Typography
Inter is used for all text to maintain enterprise readability. 
- Headings are bold and tracked tightly. 
- Body text is optimized for data density (14px base).

## Layout & Components
- **Imagery:** Must feature ultra-premium HD images.
- **Cards:** Crisp borders, very subtle standard box-shadows (no massive glows). Modest border-radius (8px - 12px).
- **Buttons:** Solid colors, slightly rounded corners (not pill-shaped), clear focus states.
