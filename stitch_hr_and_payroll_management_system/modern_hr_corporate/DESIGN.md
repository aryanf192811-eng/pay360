## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, progressive corporate aesthetic tailored for next-generation workforce management and enterprise human resources. It merges executive professionalism with warm, approachable elegance. 

The visual style blends **Modern Corporate** structure with **Soft Lilac Glassmorphism** and high-contrast tonal containment. Soft, airy lilac surfaces establish a tranquil, human-centric workspace, contrasted against deep midnight charcoal containers that anchor strategic insights and key data. The emotional intent is clear, trustworthy, and sophisticated—replacing cold corporate bureaucracy with thoughtful digital craftsmanship, generous curvature, and luminous atmospheric gradients.

## Colors

The palette leverages a signature interplay between delicate lavender tones and grounded obsidian slate:

- **Primary (`#766e9c`)**: A muted, sophisticated amethyst-slate used for primary active states, prominent action pills, and key interactive highlights.
- **Secondary (`#1e1e24`)**: Deep charcoal/midnight slate providing visual anchor blocks, high-contrast dark sections, and high-impact typographic contrast.
- **Tertiary (`#b3a5d9`)**: Soft periwinkle lilac, perfect for badges, light overlays, pill chips, and glow rings.
- **Neutral (`#f5f3fa`)**: A pristine, slightly warm lilac-white base that avoids clinical sterile white, supported by intermediate layers (`#ece8f5` for containers, `#faf8ff` for clean cards).

### Contextual Usage
- **Light Sections**: Use `#faf8ff` or `#f5f3fa` base backgrounds with `#1e1e24` body text and `#766e9c` subtle subheadings.
- **Dark Sections**: Inverted dark blocks use `#1e1e24` backgrounds with nested elevated tiles at `#26242e` and `#312e3b`, framed by `#ffffff` and `#b3a5d9` typography.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** serves as the unified geometric foundation across all hierarchy levels. Its generous x-height, refined circular curves, and crisp geometric terminals echo both high-tech precision and human empathy.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Hero & Key Headings**: Styled with wide tracking reductions (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` to `800`) to evoke a grand editorial presence. Headings in dark sections lean into full uppercase or structured sentence case with clean punctuation.
- **Micro-Labels & Overlines**: Rendered in uppercase via `label-caps` with expanded tracking (`0.08em`) to act as clean visual markers over section titles.
- **Metrics**: Data callouts leverage `stat-metric` to showcase impactful numerical values (+56, 1700+) with balanced optical alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The system is constructed upon a 12-column responsive grid contained within a maximum width of `80rem` (1280px), framed by comfortable padding to maintain readability.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Alternating Thematic Blocks**: The design alternates between airy lilac/lavender light sections and heavy, full-width dark charcoal container capsules.
- **Inset Island Cards**: Key dark sections sit within high-radius outer enclosures (`rounded-3xl`), pulling the edges inward on desktop layouts (`layout-margin-desktop`) to create a floating module effect.
- **Reflow & Breakpoints**:
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters. Multi-column metric and service cards align in 4-column or 3-column rows.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 6-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters; service cards collapse to 2x2 grids.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column single-stack flow with `1rem` gutters; outer padding tightens to `1.25rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through a hybrid model of **tonal layering**, **soft ambient shadows**, and **frosted glass accents** rather than high-contrast structural borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface 0 (Base Canvas)**: Tinted lilac-white (`#f5f3fa` to `#faf8ff`).
- **Surface 1 (Card & Module)**: Crisp white cards (`#ffffff`) or frosted translucent lilac (`rgba(245, 243, 250, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **Surface 2 (Dark Inset Canvas)**: Deep slate container (`#1e1e24`).
- **Surface 3 (Dark Elevated Card)**: Tonal tiers resting inside dark sections (`#26242e` and `#312e3b`).

### Shadow Metrics
- **Subtle Ambient**: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(118, 110, 156, 0.08)`—diffused with a faint purple undertone to maintain palette coherence.
- **Floating Pill**: `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.12)`.
- **Inner Depth**: Inset badges use gentle glass highlight lines: `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` over dark regions and `border: 1px solid rgba(118, 110, 156, 0.12)` over light regions.

## Shapes

The design adopts a generous, curvilinear shape identity (`level 3: Pill-shaped / High-Curvature`).

- **Base Cards & Modules**: Utilize `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem) or `rounded-3xl` (2rem to 2.5rem) on major container wrappers to soften transitions and emphasize friendliness.
- **Buttons & Action Badges**: Strict pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) ensures tactile clarity and an executive finish.
- **Corner Accents**: Feature interactive cards with asymmetrical rounded accents (e.g., circular action chips docked in the top-right notch of card containers).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill**: Filled with `#766e9c` (or `#1e1e24` on light backgrounds), pure white typography (`label-md`), full border radius (`rounded-full`), and an inline circle icon badge on the right. Padding: `0.625rem 1.25rem`.
- **Secondary Glass Pill**: Translucent lilac background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` on light, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` on dark), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Circular Icon Button**: 40px to 48px round button with light gray-lilac fill (`#ece8f5`), containing directional arrows or media controls.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Badge**: Pill-shaped with soft lilac background (`#ece8f5`), text in `#766e9c`, micro size (`label-caps`).
- **Dark-Section Badge**: Translucent white fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), text `#ffffff`, minimal border stroke.

### Cards
- **Light Metric Card**: Soft white canvas (`#ffffff`), `rounded-2xl` corners, ambient purple-tinted drop shadow, internal layout featuring oversized stat metric on top, followed by muted descriptor text.
- **Dark Feature Card**: Surface `#26242e` or `#312e3b`, `rounded-2xl`, internal padding `1.75rem`. Features an elevated circular icon, bold white title, and muted lilac body copy.
- **Notched Action Card**: Card featuring a floating circular arrow button pinned to the upper right corner with a subtle inset margin.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Input Bars**: Integrated pill shapes (`rounded-full`), background tinted with `#ece8f5` or frosted white glass. Text in `#1e1e24`, placeholder in `#766e9c`. Trailing circular submit action button.

### Lists & Row Items
- Stacked within `rounded-2xl` containers, separated by subtle dividers (`rgba(118, 110, 156, 0.1)`). Rows include numerical index indicators in bold pill format (`01/`, `02/`).
